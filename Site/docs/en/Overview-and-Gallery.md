# Overview and gallery

## The overview

The overview is the home page: a set of panels laid out in one to five columns. The arrows at the
top left change the column count. Press **Edit** to rearrange everything; **Done** leaves the mode.

The default panels are:

- **Quick access** — the folders pinned to the sidebar.
- **Frequent** — the folders you open most often, with a visit count.
- **Recent** — the folders and files you opened most recently.
- **Home** — your home folder, as a table or a grid.
- **Drives** — every mounted volume with its free space.
- **Shared** — the folders this device shares, and the ones the paired devices offer.
- **Favorites** — the files and folders marked with **Add to favorites** in the context menu.

A panel can be a **folder** too: pick any directory and it is shown in place.

## Editing panels

While the overview is in edit mode:

- **Add panel** appends a new panel of any kind.
- The icon button changes a panel's icon.
- The title becomes a text box; press `Enter` or click away to rename it.
- The pin button keeps the panel in the sidebar as well.
- List/grid switches that panel's layout, the arrows move it, and `×` removes it.

The arrows reorder the panels, and the layout is stored in the settings.

## Quick access

Quick access is the list of folders in the sidebar. Drag a folder onto the sidebar to add it, or use
the overview's edit mode. Right-click an entry to rename it, change its icon or remove it; the name
and icon are stored per path and survive restarts.

## The gallery

The gallery scans the folders listed in [Settings](Settings-and-Shortcuts) → Gallery, three levels
deep, and groups every picture it finds by day. Double-click a tile to open it, and use the refresh
button to scan again.

Nothing is copied or moved: the gallery only reads the files and stores thumbnails in the app's
cache. Adding a folder is a matter of choosing it in the settings; removing it stops future scans.
